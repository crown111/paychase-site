PayCollie · Herds your unpaid invoices home.

Get paid on overdue invoices, automatically.

PayCollie is an iPhone app for independent businesses. It connects to your Stripe or QuickBooks account, tracks unpaid invoices, and sends friendly, escalating reminders by email and text, each with a one-tap link to pay.

  • Reminders that escalateDay 7, 14 and 30 reminders, plus an instant nudge when a subscription card is declined.
  • One-tap pay linksCustomers pay on Stripe's or QuickBooks' own secure invoice page. PayCollie never touches the money.
  • Stops the moment you're paidEvery reminder re-checks the invoice first, so nobody who already paid gets nagged.
  • Read-only accessPayCollie only reads invoices. It can't move money, issue refunds or change your account.
